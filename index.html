What we found / 1 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Slow first homepage view.

October 20’s simulated slow-4G homepage test showed main content at 20.7 seconds. A difficult connection may lose a visitor before booking. Separate 28-day site-wide real-visit data passed Google’s checks: 0.9 seconds. Google speed report ↗

02

A confusing first choice.

Booking offers location, provider and first-available choices, with little guidance first. Patients ready to enquire may leave if they do not know which route fits. Booking entry ↗

03

Review links lose the source.

Five sampled “Read More” links point back to the homepage. Patients must search again to check your reputation. The 452-review widget may differ in scope from Google; age is unknown. Review section ↗

What we found / 2 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Feedback and giveaway routes mix.

The feedback page connects reviews or feedback with a treatment draw. Patients may be unsure which they are entering. Routes were not fully tested; no policy violation established. Feedback page ↗

05

Contact details differ.

Your footer says Sacramento 95862; LinkedIn says 95825. El Dorado Hills site and Google phones also differ. Patients may second-guess the contact details. Phones may be intentional call tracking; verify routing and the ZIP. Shopping-center listing ↗

06

Four tag managers load.

Four Google Tag Manager containers appear on the homepage. Overlapping counts, if present, could misguide ad decisions. Duplicate events and any speed impact remain unverified. Website check ↗

01
